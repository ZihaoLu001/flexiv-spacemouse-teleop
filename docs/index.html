ROS 2 Humble · MoveIt Servo · Flexiv Rizon

Flexiv SpaceMouse Teleop

A SpaceMouse teleoperation bridge for Flexiv Rizon arms: 6-DoF twist commands into MoveIt Servo with deadman gating, GN01 gripper buttons, a version-controlled Servo configuration, and one-command startup. 

# Fake hardware (no robot needed)
scripts/teleop.sh

# Real robot
ROBOT_SN=Rizon4s-062626 scripts/teleop.sh --real

Hold button 0 as the deadman to move the arm, press button 1 to toggle the gripper, and Ctrl-C to shut the whole stack down.
